Privacy Policy

Lemonade Stand: Squeeze Street · Last updated October 29, 2026
The short version
  • No accounts, no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device. We never receive it.
  • The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers to show and measure ads. You can say no to tracking.
  • No in-app purchases. We do not sell personal information.

1. What is stored on your device

Lemonade Stand: Squeeze Street ("the game", "we") is a single-player game. To keep your progress, the game saves the following in your phone's standard app storage:

  • Game progress: in-game cash, day, level, supplies, recipe, flavor, price, upgrades, locations, goals, trophies, crates, stickers, franchise stars, challenge scores and records.
  • Settings: sound on or off, game speed, whether reminder notifications were offered.
  • Simple counters used inside the game, such as how many times the game was opened (so a "rate this game" prompt appears rarely and never repeatedly).

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us. Deleting the app, or using More → Start over, erases it permanently. Because we never have a copy, we cannot restore it.

2. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example, that your stand earned money while you were away). They are created and stored locally; no notification data is sent to us. You can turn them off anytime in your phone's Settings.

3. Sharing

When you tap Share or Brag to friends, the game passes a text and/or image of your results to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice and is handled by the app you pick. We receive nothing.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. It shows full-screen ads at natural breaks (at most about once every five minutes, never during a selling day) and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch for a bonus.

To serve, limit, measure and protect ads from fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

Your choices

  • iPhone: before any ad loads, the game asks for permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA is not used. Change it anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK, Switzerland and other places where it is required: Google's consent form appears before personalized ads. You can change your choice anytime in More → Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

If you decline, you may still see ads, but they are not personalized. Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not run our own analytics or servers for the game.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested for a general audience. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Because the game keeps your progress only on your device and we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. For data processed by Google for advertising, use the choices in section 4 or Google's tools at myaccount.google.com.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

9. Contact and support

Questions about privacy, a bug, or an idea for the game? Email yp7buenapark@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Lost progress? Progress lives only on your phone, so it cannot be restored after the app is deleted.
