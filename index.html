Lemonade Stand: Squeeze Street

The classic lemonade stand game, reinvented.

Start with a cardboard box and $25 of lemons on a quiet street. Read the weather, mix the perfect recipe, set the right price, then watch your customers react cup by cup. Grow it into a lemonade empire, all the way to a stand on the Moon.

  • 13 locations, from Maple Lane to Moon Base, each with its own upgrades
  • 9 lemonade flavors, town cravings, influencers, food critics and rare millionaires
  • Lemon Crates and 54 stickers: finish a set for a bonus that lasts forever
  • Franchise your empire and climb from Street Vendor to Lemonade Legend
  • Challenge mode: 7, 14 or 30 days to make as much money as you can
  • Plays offline. No account needed.
