How do I play?

Each morning, check the forecast, buy supplies, pick a flavor, set your recipe and price, then tap OPEN STAND. Customers react to every cup: 😍 means you nailed it, 😖 too sour, 🥵 needs more ice, 💸 too pricey. The first launch walks you through it, and More → How to play has the full rules.

What are Lemon Crates and stickers?

You earn crates from great days, level-ups, daily check-ins, clearing all 3 daily goals and rare millionaire customers. Open them in the 👑 Empire tab for stickers. Finish a set of 6 for a bonus that lasts forever. Crates are only earned by playing and can't be bought. The odds are under Empire → Crate odds.

What does Franchise do?

From level 20 you can sell your stand for stars. Each star adds +4% to every sale and +2% XP forever. You start a new stand on Maple Lane but keep your stickers, crates, trophies, records and streak.

How does Challenge mode work?

Pick 7, 14 or 30 days. You start with $25 and every spot is open; your money at the end is your score. Medals pay a bonus into your Career. Challenges unlock once the tutorial is done.

Do I need an account or internet?

No account is needed and the game plays offline. Ads and bonus videos need a connection.

How do I reset my progress?

Progress is saved only on your phone. More → Start over erases it, and so does deleting the app. Because we never have a copy, lost progress can't be restored.
